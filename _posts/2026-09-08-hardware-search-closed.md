Two decision records on one day that pull in different directions, both kept.

- **[ADR-0001](../decisions/ADR-0001-teaching-lab-bundle.md):** the search for
  a new storage host is closed; the TS140 stays. A teaching-lab bundle is
  bought instead: two T320s, eight thin clients, two pfSense appliances.
- **[ADR-0002](../decisions/ADR-0002-storage-host-moves-to-t320.md), proposed:**
  re-reading the T320 spec sheet found the half-height media bays that the first
  reading missed. Bays, not power, had been the real constraint, and the T320
  wins on bays.

**Error recorded:** the earlier evaluation declined the T320 partly on
electricity cost. The owner has solar, so that argument was withdrawn. What
survived is that UPS runtime is physics, not billing.
