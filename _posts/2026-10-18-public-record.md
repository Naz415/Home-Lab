The private working repository is reconstructed here as a public, sanitized
academic record. The commits replay the events in order, errors included, each
followed by the commit that corrected it. Branches merge with `--no-ff` and
milestones are tagged.

**Commit dates are today's.** The event date is at the start of each commit
message. The history is a reconstruction and does not pretend otherwise. The
original commits, from 5 October onward, are in the private repository.

See the [README](https://github.com/Naz415/Home-Lab#readme) for how to read the
history, and the [sanitization policy](../SANITIZATION.md) for what was removed
and why.
